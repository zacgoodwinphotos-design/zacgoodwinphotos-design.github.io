Backup

Your log is stored on this phone only. Export it every week or two so you always have a copy. Export your whole log as a Markdown file: every set, plank hold, completed run and plan change, in plain tables you can read, edit or upload anywhere. Importing a log (Markdown, or an older .json backup) adds what's in it and replaces any matching days; nothing else is deleted.

Plank uses the built-in timer: a 5-second countdown, then beeps when you reach your target and the top of the range. Bodyweight lifts are logged at 0 kg and progress by reps.
